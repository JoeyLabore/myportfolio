Project Background

NestBank is a non-profit microfinance organisation dedicated to helping women in poverty build small businesses and achieve financial independence. Founded by Nobel Peace Prize laureate Muhammad Yunus, it provides affordable microloans, financial training, and support to women entrepreneurs across the United States, fostering economic inclusion and community growth.


  • Client: NestBank
  • Type of work: Mobile UX
  • Duration: 2022–2023
  • My Role: Senior Product Designer

Problem & Solution

Problem

NestBank’s loan management process relied heavily on in-person interactions and manual systems, limiting accessibility and scalability as membership grew across multiple states.


Solution

Designed a mobile-first platform that simplified loan applications, payments, and account management. The new app empowered members to take control of their finances while reducing operational inefficiencies.

Deliverables

  • Current state analysis & future state strategy
  • Mobile product design & prototyping
  • Design System
  • User research & usability testing
  • Visual identity

Impact

1,164,000+ loans disbursed digitally

Shows scale and financial empowerment delivered through the platform. Connects directly to impact on women entrepreneurs.

111,000+ active users

Demonstrates widespread adoption and trust in the app as the go-to financial tool for underserved communities.

97% of invited members registered

Highlights inclusivity and accessibility — nearly every woman invited is engaging with the platform, proving strong product-market fit.
