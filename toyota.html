Project Background

Toyota Racing Development (TRD) is Toyota’s motorsport division, building and supporting high-performance Nascar race engines.

Operating in a highly regulated environment, performance-driven environment, TRD coordinates engineering, manufacturing, testing, and track support to deliver engines under tight timelines.


  • Client: Toyota Racing Development
  • Industry: Motorsports
  • Duration: 2025 - Present
  • Role: Product Design Lead. I led the redesign of core workflows and platform experiences.

Problem & Solution

Problem

TRD’s 20+ year-old process became complex and fragmented, relying on tribal knowledge, lacking standardised workflows, and limiting lifecycle visibility. This led to data inconsistencies and increased operational risk.


Solution

Transformed their platform into a structured, workflow-driven system, standardising operations, improving lifecycle visibility, and reducing reliance on manual processes and tribal knowledge.

Deliverables

  • Current state analysis, system and workflow mapping
  • Future state workflows
  • Platform interface design & visual design
  • Design system integrated with AI development workflows
  • User testing and validation

Impact

Lowered risk

through clearer workflows and automated validation

Improved efficiency

by reducing manual coordination and approval delays

Increased visibility

of parts and engine status across teams

Built a scalable platform

to support future TRD systems
