Project Background

Toyota Racing Development (TRD) is Toyota’s motorsport division, responsible for designing, building, and supporting high-performance race engines across multiple racing series. Working under strict performance and regulatory constraints, TRD coordinates engineering, manufacturing, testing, and track support to deliver race-ready engines on tight timelines.


  • Client: Toyota Racing Development
  • Industry: Automotive / Motorsports
  • Duration: 2025–Present
  • My Role: Product Design Lead

Problem & Solution

Problem

OnePlan had become complex, manual, and fragmented over time, slowing teams down and increasing operational risk in a fast-paced racing environment.


Solution

We're rebuilding OnePlan on a modern platform, simplifying workflows and improving cross-team visibility to support faster, more reliable execution.

Projected Deliverables

This project spans multiple discovery and build phases as we redesign key OnePlan modules. Deliverables include a cohesive design system and visual identity, in-depth research into current and future states (journey maps, user flows, interviews, usability testing), and the creation of initial concepts through to high-fidelity prototypes.

Intended Impact

Reduce change cycle time

by identifying impacts earlier

Lower risk

by minimizing late-stage rework and disruption

Improve efficiency

by reducing manual coordination and approval bottlenecks

Support scalable growth

with a modern, extensible platform foundation
