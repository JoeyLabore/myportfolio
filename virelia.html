Project Background

Virelia is a leading healthcare education platform delivering training and compliance solutions across the United States.

In a highly regulated clinical environment, it manages a large and growing content library. As demand increased, it needed to modernise its content creation infrastructure to support scale, efficiency, and AI integration.


  • Client: Virelia
  • Industry: Healthcare Education
  • Duration: 2024–2025
  • My Role: Product Design Lead. I led the design of AI-driven content workflows and platform experiences.

Problem & Solution

Problem

Virelia sought a scalable, future-ready content model to leverage emerging technologies and lead innovation. To acomplish this, the workflow needed reimagining, enabling faster, compliant production at scale.


Solution

AI was introduced to support a 50% reduction in transcript production time, helping meet growing content demand while upholding clinical accuracy, compliance standards, and brand consistency.

Deliverables

  • Current state analysis & future state strategy
  • Platform interface design
  • Design System
  • Visual identity & direction
  • User testing & validation

Impact

  • Reduced transcript creation time by shifting from manual drafting to AI-first generation
  • Accelerated time to market for new educational content
  • Decreased rework and errors across writing, QA, and compliance
  • Enabled faster feature development through a scalable visual direction and design system
