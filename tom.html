Project Background

TOM is an AI-driven DevOps automation tool that simplifies the journey from code to cloud. Built on the TEAM framework, it helps teams plan smarter, move faster, and scale with confidence.


The brand draws inspiration from the strategic tension of Tom and Jerry — where intelligence outmaneuvers chaos. In this story, TOM represents focus, agility, and smart execution. The identity balances playfulness with precision to make DevOps feel accessible and human.

Problem & Solution

Problem

DevOps tools often feel complex, fragmented, and overly technical. Many platforms prioritize power over clarity, making automation seem intimidating rather than empowering.


Solution

TOM reframes DevOps as structured and intelligent — not chaotic.


The visual system uses bold geometric forms and a dynamic cat symbol to represent strategy, agility, and control. Through color, motion, and clear messaging, the brand communicates one idea:


Smart Planning, Smarter Outcomes.

Deliverables

  • Logo system (icon, secondary, full lockup)
  • Visual identity (color, geometry, motion principles)
  • Hero & landing page design direction
