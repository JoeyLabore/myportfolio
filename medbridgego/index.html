Project Background

MedbridgeGO is a mobile rehabilitation platform designed to help patients complete prescribed therapy programs outside the clinic while staying engaged throughout recovery.


  • Client: Medbridge
  • Industry: Digital Healthcare
  • Type of Work: Mobile Product Design
  • Duration: 2021 - 2022
  • Role: Sr UI Designer

Problem & Solution

Problem

The rehabilitation experience felt impersonal, making it difficult for patients to stay engaged and confident throughout recovery. Limited communication between patients and clinics also created friction that negatively impacted retention.


Solution

Designed a more supportive and patient-centred mobile experience with clearer exercise flows, improved usability, and a unified visual direction to help patients stay engaged and confident during recovery.

Deliverables

  • Updated Design System
  • Refined Visual Language
  • Mobile Interface Redesign
  • Interaction & Motion Updates
  • UX Flow Optimisation

Impact

Improved rehabilitation usability

More accessible patient experience

Stronger product consistency

Increased patient confidence

Higher rehabilitation completion rates
